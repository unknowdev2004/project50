About the EMI Calculator

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is a fixed payment made by a borrower to a lender each calendar month. EMI consists of two parts: principal repayment and interest. Our EMI Calculator uses the standard reducing balance formula to give you accurate monthly payment figures for home loans, car loans, and personal loans.

Formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is principal, r is monthly interest rate, and n is loan tenure in months.
