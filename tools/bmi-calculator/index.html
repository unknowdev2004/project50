About the BMI Calculator

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple numeric value derived from a person's weight and height. It is widely used as a screening tool to categorise individuals as underweight, normal weight, overweight, or obese. Our BMI Calculator supports both metric (kg/cm) and imperial (lbs/ft) units.

BMI ranges: Underweight < 18.5 · Normal 18.5–24.9 · Overweight 25–29.9 · Obese ≥ 30. Note that BMI is a population-level screening measure, not a clinical diagnosis — factors such as muscle mass, age, and ethnicity affect its accuracy.

Frequently Asked Questions

BMI is a useful population-level screening indicator but it doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, or fat distribution. Athletes may have a high BMI without excess body fat. Always consult a healthcare professional for a comprehensive assessment.
For most adults, a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered healthy. A BMI below 18.5 is classified as underweight, 25–29.9 as overweight, and 30 or above as obese. Ranges may differ for children and certain ethnic groups.
