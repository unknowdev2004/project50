About Project 50

Project 50 is a free platform containing 50 carefully selected online tools across five categories: Health & Fitness, Student Tools, Utility Tools, Finance Tools, and Creator Tools.

Every tool runs entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server. No account is required. No payment is ever needed.

Our goal is simple: give everyone access to practical, reliable tools without the friction of sign-ups, subscriptions, or advertising interruptions.
